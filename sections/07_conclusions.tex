\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

This section will be finalized after the numerical maps and ridge overlays are inserted.  The intended conclusion is that an atomically resolved transfer-matrix treatment of a ferromagnet--bilayer-graphene--superconductor junction produces a compact round-trip description of Andreev transport.  The finite bilayer acts as a coherent cavity whose resonant structure is controlled by the eigenvalues and residues of $\Mrt^{(g)}$, while the observed conductance ridges additionally require sufficient transverse-channel coherence to survive the $K$ integration.

The final version should emphasize three points: first, the interface transparency is not fitted but follows from the lattice connectivity and layer termination; second, the stable propagation basis avoids direct ill-conditioned powers of the central transfer matrix; third, the $\oneone$ and $\onetwo$ geometries provide distinct microscopic routes for Andreev reflection because the superconducting contact samples different layer components of the bilayer modes.
