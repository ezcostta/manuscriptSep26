\section{Transfer matrices and bilayer roots}
\label{app:transfer-matrices}

This appendix stores the algebra that is too detailed for the main text.  For a normal monolayer with scalar energy parameter $x$, the staggered state obeys
\begin{equation}
    L_M\phi_{m+1}=R_M\phi_m,
\end{equation}
with
\begin{align}
    L_M&=
    \begin{pmatrix}
        x & \xi\\
        \eta^* & 0
    \end{pmatrix},
    &
    R_M&=
    \begin{pmatrix}
        0 & -\eta\\
        -\xi^* & -x
    \end{pmatrix},
    \notag\\
    T_M&=L_M^{-1}R_M.
\end{align}

For the normal AB bilayer with $x_j=\epsilon-V_j$, the recurrence is
\begin{equation}
    L_{\rm BL}\Psi_{m+1}=R_{\rm BL}\Psi_m,
    \qquad
    T_{\rm BL}=L_{\rm BL}^{-1}R_{\rm BL},
\end{equation}
where
\begin{align}
    L_{\rm BL}
    &=
    \begin{pmatrix}
        x_1 & \xi & 0 & 0\\
        \eta^* & 0 & 0 & 0\\
        0 & 0 & x_2 & \xi\\
        t_\perp & 0 & \eta^* & 0
    \end{pmatrix},
    \label{eq:TBL-L-app}\\
    R_{\rm BL}
    &=
    \begin{pmatrix}
        0 & -\eta & 0 & -t_\perp\\
        -\xi^* & -x_1 & 0 & 0\\
        0 & 0 & 0 & -\eta\\
        0 & 0 & -\xi^* & -x_2
    \end{pmatrix}.
    \label{eq:TBL-LR-app}
\end{align}
A useful implementation check is
\begin{equation}
    \det T_{\rm BL}=\left(\frac{\eta}{\eta^*}\right)^2=e^{2iK},
\end{equation}
valid away from $\eta(K)=0$.

Using the Bloch form $A_j(m)=U_{Aj}e^{im\vartheta}$, $B_j(m)=U_{Bj}e^{im\vartheta}$ and defining
\begin{equation}
    f(\vartheta,K)=\eta(K)+\xi e^{i\vartheta},
    \qquad
    s=|f|^2,
\end{equation}
the bilayer secular equation is
\begin{equation}
    (x_1^2-s)(x_2^2-s)-t_\perp^2x_1x_2=0.
\end{equation}
The two solutions are
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
    s_\nu(\epsilon)
    ={}&
    \frac{1}{2}
    \left[
        x_1^2+x_2^2
        +\nu\sqrt{(x_1^2-x_2^2)^2+4t_\perp^2x_1x_2}
    \right],
    \\
    &\nu=\pm1.
\end{split}
\end{equation}
Writing $\vartheta=K/2+Q$, one obtains
\begin{equation}
    s=t^2\left[3+2\cos K+4\cos(K/2)\cos Q\right],
\end{equation}
which leads to the roots quoted in Eq.~\eqref{eq:bilayer-roots}.
