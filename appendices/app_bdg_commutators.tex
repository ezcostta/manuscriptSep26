\section{BdG commutators and pairing convention}
\label{app:bdg-commutators}

This appendix records the convention needed to keep the two spin-singlet BdG sectors in the same algebraic form.  In a complete one-particle basis, including spin, the pairing matrix is antisymmetric,
\begin{equation}
    \Delta_{ij}=-\Delta_{ji}.
    \label{eq:Delta-antisymmetry-app}
\end{equation}
For onsite $s$-wave pairing the spatial, layer, and sublattice labels coincide, while the two spin labels are opposite; the spatial part is symmetric, but the full spin-orbital matrix remains antisymmetric.

For the general quadratic pairing term
\begin{equation}
    \hat H_\Delta
    =
    \frac12\sum_{ij}
    \left(
        \Delta_{ij}\hat c_i^\dagger\hat c_j^\dagger
        +
        \Delta_{ij}^*\hat c_j\hat c_i
    \right),
\end{equation}
the creation part gives
\begin{align}
    \left[
        \frac12\sum_{ij}\Delta_{ij}\hat c_i^\dagger\hat c_j^\dagger,
        v_l\hat c_l
    \right]
    &=
    \frac12\sum_i v_l\left(\Delta_{il}-\Delta_{li}\right)\hat c_i^\dagger
    \\
    &=
    \sum_i \Delta_{il}v_l\hat c_i^\dagger,
    \label{eq:creation-pairing-comm}
\end{align}
where Eq.~\eqref{eq:Delta-antisymmetry-app} was used.  The conjugate pairing part gives a contribution proportional to
\begin{equation}
    \sum_i \Delta^*_{il}u_i\hat c_l.
    \label{eq:conjugate-pairing-index}
\end{equation}
Thus the index order in the hole equation is fixed by
\begin{equation}
    (\Delta^\dagger)_{li}=\Delta^*_{il}.
    \label{eq:Ddagger-index}
\end{equation}

With the quasiparticle operator of Eq.~\eqref{eq:qp-operator}, the $s_\sigma$ factor in the hole component cancels the spin-dependent sign of the singlet commutator.  After this cancellation the sector equations can be written as
\begin{align}
    E u_k
    &=
    \sum_l h_{kl}u_l+
    \sum_i \Delta_{ki}v_i,
    \\
    E v_l
    &=
    -\sum_i h_{il}v_i+
    \sum_i \Delta^*_{il}u_i.
    \label{eq:bdg-component-equations-app}
\end{align}
Equation~\eqref{eq:bdg-component-equations-app} is the component form of the BdG block matrix in Eq.~\eqref{eq:BdG-block}.
